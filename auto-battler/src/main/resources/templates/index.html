How it works

Step 1

Recruit and equip

Pick heroes from the catalog, give them skills and equipment.

Step 2

Build a team

Choose up to four different heroes who work well together.

Step 3

Pick a dungeon

Each unlocked dungeon holds a group of monsters and a boss.

Step 4

Watch the battle

The fight is simulated automatically. Read the battle log turn by turn.

Step 5

Grow stronger

Victories give experience and gold, and unlock new heroes and dungeons.

The basics

Heroes

Every hero has a role (warrior, mage, rogue or healer) and its own health, attack, defense and speed. Speed decides who acts first in a turn.

Heroes gain levels with experience, which raises all their stats.

Skills and equipment

Skills can attack, heal, shield or poison. Equipment boosts stats and fits in one of three slots: weapon, armor or accessory.

A hero cannot wear two items in the same slot.

Dungeons and monsters

Monsters are normal, elite or boss. A dungeon never has more than one boss, and its difficulty sets the rewards.

Some heroes and dungeons start locked and must be earned.

Rules at a glance

  • A team holds one to four heroes, with no duplicates.
  • A battle needs a team with at least one hero and an unlocked dungeon.
  • A battle ends when all monsters or all heroes are defeated.
  • After 50 turns without a winner, the battle counts as a defeat.
  • Every battle is saved in your history, with its full log.
